# Onboarding flow

1. **Pick stream.** Present Science PCB, Science PCM, Commerce, and Humanities.
2. **Pick subjects.** Load the matching `subjects/<stream>.json`. Require English. Science requires Physics and Chemistry, then Biology or Mathematics, plus one or two allowed options. Commerce requires Accountancy, Business Studies, and Economics, plus one or two options. Humanities requires English plus any four allowed subjects.
3. **Pick preferences.** Save English, Hindi, or Hinglish; CBSE or State Board; delivery time with a default of 07:00; and questions per day with a default of one per subject.

Before every send, filter the bank by the saved subject IDs. If a subject is missing, never send it. Skill subjects should produce a one-mark, practical, diagram, map, or identification item rather than an oversized theory question.
